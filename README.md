# Provia

### Your profile. Your potential. Precisely presented.

**Provia** is an AI-powered resume tailoring platform designed to turn a
generic profile into a resume that speaks directly to the opportunity
you're targeting.

Instead of creating the same resume for every application, Provia
analyzes the **job role, company, required skills, and preferences**,
then uses AI to identify the strongest matches in your profile and
generate a focused, professional resume around them.

## What makes Provia different?

-   🎯 **Job-specific tailoring**: Every resume is shaped around a
    specific opportunity.
-   🤖 **AI-powered analysis**: Identifies relevant skills, experience,
    projects, and keywords.
-   🧩 **Smart matching**: Connects your existing profile evidence with
    what the role actually needs.
-   ✍️ **AI-assisted writing**: Reworks resume content while staying
    grounded in the information you provide.
-   🎨 **Professional templates**: Create polished, structured resumes
    with customizable designs.
-   📄 **Ready to share**: Export resumes as PDF or JPG and print them
    in A4 format.
-   🔄 **Multiple targets**: Create different tailored resumes for
    different roles and companies.

## The idea

**One profile. Multiple opportunities. A resume built for each one.**

Provia is built around a simple principle: your resume shouldn't just
describe you. It should make the **most relevant version of your
experience visible for the opportunity in front of you.**
